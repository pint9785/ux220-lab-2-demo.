The Best Thing I've Read Lately

Recently I have read and enjoyed the book, The Design of Everyday Things by Don Norman. It is a book I have heard great things about and decided to read before school started as it is well liked in the design community. While reading it, I learned a lot about how things that we use on a daily basis can have a poor design that makes it harder for users and their relationship with said object. As designers, our whole goal is to create good designs that are functional for users. This book was helpful to read as a designer as it gives good insight on user-centered design and creating with human psychology in mind. I would personally recommend it to design students and anyone who likes learning about accessibility in everyday objects.
